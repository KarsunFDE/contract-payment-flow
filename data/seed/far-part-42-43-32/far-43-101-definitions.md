# FAR 43.101 — Definitions (STARTER STUB)

"Contract modification" means any written change in the terms of a contract
(see 43.103). A **unilateral modification** is signed only by the contracting
officer; it is used to make administrative changes, issue change orders, make
changes authorized by clauses other than a changes clause (e.g., Property
clause, Options clause), and issue termination notices. A **bilateral
modification** (supplemental agreement) is signed by both the contractor and
the contracting officer; it is used to make negotiated equitable adjustments,
definitize letter contracts, and reflect other agreements modifying the terms
of contracts.
