DFARS Part 243 — Contract Modifications

Subpart 243.1 — General

243.107 [Reserved]

243.107-70 Notification of substantial impact on employment.

The Secretary of Defense must notify the Secretary of Labor when a major defense contract or subcontract modification substantially impacts employment. The clause at 249.7003(c) requires contractors to notify employees, subcontractors, and state/local officials of such impacts.

Subpart 243.2 — Change Orders

243.204 Administration.

Follow PGI 243.204 procedures for change order administration.

243.204-70 Definitization of change orders.

243.204-70-1 Scope.

This applies to unpriced change orders exceeding $5 million in estimated value. Unpriced change orders for foreign military sales and special access programs should follow these procedures to the maximum extent practicable, with prior notice to the Office of the Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy if impracticable.

243.204-70-2 Price ceiling.

Unpriced change orders must include a not-to-exceed price.

243.204-70-3 Definitization schedule.

Definitization must occur by the earlier of: (1) 180 days after issuance (extendable only to 180 days after contractor proposal submission), or (2) when obligated funds equal more than 50% of the not-to-exceed price. Timely qualifying proposal submission is a material contract element.

243.204-70-4 Limitations on obligations.

The Government may not obligate more than 50% of the not-to-exceed price before definitization, unless the contractor submits a qualifying proposal earlier, allowing up to 75% obligation (per 232.102-70).

243.204-70-5 Exceptions.

Limitations do not apply to: (1) initial spares purchases, (2) ship construction/repair (regarding 243.204-70-4(a)), or (3) when the agency head waives them for contingency, humanitarian, or peacekeeping operations.

243.204-70-6 Allowable Profit.

When final pricing occurs after substantial performance completion, profit must reflect reduced cost risk, actual cost incurrence, and performance timing (see 215.404-71-3(d)(2)).

243.204-70-7 Plans and reports.

Departments must include unpriced change order management actions in the Consolidated UCA Management Plan and report each action exceeding $5 million in the Consolidated UCA Management Report (per 217.7405).

243.204-71 Certification of requests for equitable adjustment.

Requests exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold require contractor certification per 252.243-7002. Calculate thresholds by adding absolute values of all cost increases and decreases. Conversions to claims under the Contract Disputes statute require certification per FAR subpart 33.2.

243.205 Contract clauses.

243.205-70 Pricing of contract modifications.

Use clause 252.243-7001 in fixed-price contract solicitations and contracts.

243.205-71 Requests for equitable adjustment.

Use clause 252.243-7002 in solicitations and contracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, including FAR part 12 commercial acquisitions.

243.205-72 Unpriced change orders.

See 217.7406 clause prescriptions for unpriced change orders exceeding $5 million estimated value.
