52.232-25 Prompt Payment.

As prescribed in 32.908(c), insert the following clause:

Prompt Payment (Jan 2017)

(a) Invoice payments.

(1) Due date. Payment is due the later of:

(A) 30 days after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the Contractor (except as provided in paragraph (a)(1)(ii) of this clause); or

(B) 30 days after Government acceptance of supplies delivered or services performed.

If the designated billing office fails to annotate the invoice with the actual date of receipt at the time of receipt, the invoice payment due date shall be the 30th day after the date of the Contractor's invoice, provided a proper invoice is received and there is no disagreement over quantity, quality, or Contractor compliance with contract requirements.

(2) Certain food products and other payments. Accelerated payment applies to meat, fish, perishable agricultural commodities, and dairy products-as close as possible to but not later than 7 days after delivery for meat and fish, 10 days for perishable agricultural items and dairy products.

(3) Contractor's invoice. To constitute a proper invoice, the invoice must include the following information and comply with any applicable regulations:

(i) Name and address of the Contractor.

(ii) Invoice date and invoice number.

(iii) Contract number or other authorization for supplies delivered or services performed (including order number and line item number).

(iv) Description, quantity, unit of measure, unit price, and extended price of supplies delivered or services performed.

(v) Shipping and payment terms (e.g., shipment number and date of shipment, discount for prompt payment terms).

(vi) Name and address of Contractor official to whom payment is to be sent (must be the same as that in the contract or in a proper notice of assignment).

(vii) Name (where practicable), title, phone number, and mailing address of person to notify in the event of a defective invoice.

(viii) Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN), if required.

(ix) Electronic funds transfer (EFT) banking information, if required.

The designated billing office will return an improper invoice, within 7 days after receipt (3 days for meat/fish, 5 days for perishable agricultural items/dairy products), explaining the reasons why it is not a proper invoice.

(4) Interest penalty. The designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, without request from the Contractor, if payment is not made by the due date and the conditions of paragraphs (a)(4)(i) through (iii) of this clause are met, if applicable.

(5) Computing penalty amount. The Government will compute interest penalties in accordance with the Office of Management and Budget prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR Part 1315.

Government acceptance is deemed to occur constructively on the 7th day after the Contractor delivers supplies or performs services in accordance with the terms and conditions of the contract, unless there is a disagreement over quantity, quality, or Contractor compliance with a contract provision.

(6) Discounts for prompt payment. The designated payment office will pay an interest penalty automatically, if the Government takes a discount for prompt payment improperly.

(7) Additional interest penalty. The designated payment office will pay a penalty amount, in addition to the interest penalty amount, only if an interest penalty of $1 or more is owed and the office does not pay the interest penalty within 10 days after the date the invoice amount is paid, and the contractor makes a written demand within 40 days of invoice payment.

(b) Contract financing payment. If the contract provides for contract financing, payments shall be made in accordance with the applicable contract financing clause.

(c) Fast payment procedure due dates. If the contract contains the clause at 52.213-1, Fast Payment Procedure, payments will be made within 15 days after the date of receipt of the invoice.

(d) Overpayments. If the Contractor becomes aware of a duplicate contract financing or invoice payment or that the Government has otherwise overpaid on a contract financing or invoice payment, the Contractor shall remit the overpaid amount to the payment office.

(End of clause)

Alternate I (Feb 2002) — Cost-reimbursement contracts for services:

(e) Invoices for interim payments.

Paragraphs (a)(2), (a)(3), (a)(4)(ii), (a)(4)(iii), and (a)(5)(i) do not apply.

The due date for making invoice payments by the designated payment office is 30 days after the designated billing office receives a proper invoice from the Contractor.

The Contractor shall submit invoices for interim payments in accordance with paragraph (a) of the clause at 52.216-7, Allowable Cost and Payment.

If the Contractor submits a proper invoice for an interim payment and the Government is unable to make payment within the 30-day period, interest penalties are owed per paragraph (a)(4) of this clause.

Improper invoices are returned within 7 days of receipt.
