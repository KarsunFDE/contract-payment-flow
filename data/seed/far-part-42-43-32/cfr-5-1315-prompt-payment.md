# 5 CFR Part 1315 — Prompt Payment (STARTER STUB)

§ 1315.4 Prompt payment standards and required notices to vendors.

(a) **Required documentation.** Agency procedures must ensure that agencies pay
only for goods and services received, and that a proper invoice has been
received.

(b) **Receipt of invoice.** The 30-day payment clock begins when the
designated billing office receives a proper invoice.

(g) **Improper invoices.** Agencies must return an improper invoice to the
vendor **within 7 days** after receipt, identifying the defects that make it
improper. If the agency fails to return the invoice within 7 days, the number
of days available to make payment without an interest penalty is reduced by the
number of days beyond the 7-day return window.

§ 1315.10 Late payment interest penalties accrue automatically when payment is
made after the due date for a proper invoice.
