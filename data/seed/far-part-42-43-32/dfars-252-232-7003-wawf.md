# DFARS 252.232-7003 — Electronic Submission via WAWF (STARTER STUB)

(b) The Contractor shall submit payment requests and receiving reports using
**Wide Area WorkFlow (WAWF)**, except in limited circumstances. A "payment
request" means any request for contract financing payment or invoice payment.
A "receiving report" means the data required for acceptance of supplies and
services (electronic equivalent of the DD Form 250 / SF-1449 acceptance).

WAWF matches the contractor's invoice (e.g., a 2-in-1 voucher, a combo, or a
cost voucher) to the corresponding receiving report so the COR/acceptor and the
paying office (DFAS) act on a consistent, auditable record. This three-way
match (contract / invoice / receiving report) underpins the proper-invoice
determination in FAR 32.905.
