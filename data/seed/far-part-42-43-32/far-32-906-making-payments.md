32.906 Making payments.

(a) General. The Government will not make invoice payments earlier than 7 days prior to the due dates specified in the contract unless the agency head determines to make earlier payment on a case-by-case basis or that accelerated payment methods are necessary.

(b) Payment office. The designated payment office will-

(1) Mail checks on the same day they are dated;

(2) For EFT payments, specify a date on or before the established due date for settlement at a Federal Reserve Bank;

(3) Make payment on the following working day without incurring late payment interest penalty when the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday when Government offices are closed; and

(4) When the designated billing office erroneously rejected a proper invoice, enter the original invoice receipt date in the payment system for calculating the correct payment due date and any interest penalties.

(c) Partial deliveries. Contracting officers must write contract statements of work and pricing arrangements allowing contractors to deliver and receive invoice payments for discrete portions of work as soon as completed and accepted. Unless specifically prohibited, the clause at 52.232-1 provides contractors are entitled to payment for accepted partial deliveries of supplies or partial performance of services complying with applicable contract requirements.

(d) Contractor identifier. Each payment or remittance advice will use the contractor invoice number along with Government or contract information describing any payment made.

(e) Discounts. When a discount for prompt payment is taken, the designated payment office will make payment as close as possible to, but not later than, the end of the discount period, calculated from the contractor's proper invoice date.
