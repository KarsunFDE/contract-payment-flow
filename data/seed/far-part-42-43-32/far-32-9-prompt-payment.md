# FAR Subpart 32.9 — Prompt Payment (STARTER STUB)

This subpart prescribes policies, procedures, and clauses for implementing the
Prompt Payment Act (31 U.S.C. chapter 39) and Office of Management and Budget
(OMB) prompt payment regulations at 5 CFR part 1315.

Agencies must pay contractors by the **due date**, which is generally the
later of 30 days after the designated billing office receives a **proper
invoice**, or 30 days after Government acceptance of supplies/services
(acceptance is deemed to occur on the date the proper invoice is received if
the contract does not specify otherwise, subject to the acceptance period).
If payment is late, the Government owes an **interest penalty** computed at the
Treasury rate. Contract financing payments and certain other payment types are
excluded from interest-penalty provisions.
