# FAR 43.301 — Use of Standard Form 30 (STARTER STUB)

(a)(1) The contracting officer shall use the SF-30, Amendment of
Solicitation/Modification of Contract, to amend solicitations, change purchase
orders, and modify contracts. Block 13 of the SF-30 identifies the authority
for the modification (e.g., the Changes clause for a unilateral change order,
or "mutual agreement of the parties" for a bilateral supplemental agreement).

The SF-30 records, among other things: the contract/order number being
modified, the modification number, the effective date, the authority for the
modification, and a description of the change and any associated change in
funding (obligation) or period of performance.
