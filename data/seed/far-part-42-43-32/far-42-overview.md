# FAR Part 42 — Contract Administration (STARTER STUB)

Part 42 prescribes policies and procedures for assigning and performing
contract administration and contract audit services. Key roles:

- **Contracting Officer (CO).** Retains authority to obligate funds, sign
  modifications, and make final contract decisions.
- **Contracting Officer's Representative (COR).** Designated in writing by the
  CO (FAR 42.302 / 1.602-2(d)) to monitor performance, accept deliverables and
  receiving reports, and review invoices. The COR may **not** make commitments
  or changes that affect price, quality, quantity, delivery, or other terms.
- **DCAA (Defense Contract Audit Agency).** Provides contract audit services,
  including evaluation of cost-type vouchers and incurred-cost submissions, and
  flags unallowable costs (FAR 31.205) and defective pricing.

Subpart 42.15 covers contractor performance evaluation (CPARS), and Subpart
42.7 covers indirect cost rates relevant to cost-reimbursement invoices.
